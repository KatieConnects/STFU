# Page 1 Setup Plan (Professional + Personality)

Use this as your first page blueprint. Fill in the bracketed parts with your own words.

## 1) Page Structure (simple)

1. **Header/Title**
2. **Tagline (one sentence)**
3. **Short Bio (3–5 lines)**
4. **What I Do (3 bullets)**
5. **Call to Action (one button or one sentence)**

---

## 3) Fast Writing Formula (for your custom bio)

Use this 3-line formula:

1. **Who you are**: “I’m [name], a [role/identity].”
2. **Who you help**: “I help [audience] [result].”
3. **How you help**: “I do this through [tutorials/tools/content].”

Example:
> I’m Katie, a practical creator and guide for people who want simple tech solutions.
> I help everyday users find easier ways to stream, save money, and learn without stress.
> I do this through clear walkthroughs, curated resources, and straightforward support.

---

## 4) Design Notes (to look professional)

- Keep the first page to **one clear message**.
- Use **short paragraphs** and lots of white space.
- Stick to **1 heading font + 1 body font**.
- Keep color palette simple (1 primary color + neutrals).
- Use one strong button above the fold (top of page).
