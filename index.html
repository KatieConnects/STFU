How to Install Apps on Your Google ONN Device

Follow these steps to allow your ONN Google TV device to install APK apps.

  1. Go to Settings on your ONN device.
  2. Select Privacy.
  3. Go to Security.
  4. Scroll over to right Turn off "Google Play Protect.
  5. Click the "home button" on remote.
  6. Top of homepage click APPS.
  7. Scroll down to:App Categories.
  8. Select ToolsInstall downloader app.
  9. Open downloader type the url for this page: https://katieconnects.github.io/STFU/.
  10. Click "Go" and scroll down to install apps.
Tip: After installing, delete the APK file inside Downloader to save storage space.
