      How to Set Up IPTV Simple Client in Kodi with M3U Playlist to watch Live TV channels in Kodi

      1. Open Kodi and go to Settings (gear icon).
      2. Select Add-ons.
      3. Click Install from repository.
      4. Open PVR Clients.
      5. Select IPTV Simple Client.
      6. Click Install.
      7. After installation, click Configure.
      8. Under General:
        • Set Location to Remote path (Internet address)
        • Enter your (US Main includes 1400 channels) Enter this where it says M3U playlist: https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/countries/us.m3u FOR SPANISH CHANNELS : https;//iptv-org.github.io/iptv/languages/spa.m3u (1734 channels) for the MAIN playlist of channels use URL: https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/index.m3u
        9. Note: After entering your playlist URL and clicking OK, it may appear as if nothing happens. This is normal.

        To finish loading channels:
        1. Exit Kodi completely.
        2. Go to Settings → Apps → Kodi.
        3. Select Force Stop, then reopen Kodi.
        4. From the Kodi home screen, scroll down to TV.
        5. In the upper-right corner, select Channels to begin loading channels.
